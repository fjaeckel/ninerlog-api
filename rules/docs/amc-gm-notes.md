# AMC/GM and cross-reference notes

Summaries in our own words of EASA Acceptable Means of Compliance (AMC), Guidance Material
(GM) and related provisions that bear on how the catalogue counts. AMC and GM are never
copied into this module (see `sources/README.md`); each note names the item and the ED
Decision so the reader can look it up at EASA. These notes were kept in the headers of
`sources/easa/*.md` until 2026-09-27 and moved here so that `sources/` holds only
verbatim texts of allowed origin.

## FCL.035 Crediting of flight time and theoretical knowledge

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-035.md`.

- GM1 FCL.035(b)(6)(ii) (ED Decision 2025/002/R) concerns theoretical knowledge credit only; no effect on flight-time counting.

## FCL.060 Recent experience

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-060.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.060(b)(1) (ED Decision 2011/016/R): an instructor or examiner on board flights flown to regain the FCL.060(b)(1) recency is not counted as a passenger.
- GM1 FCL.060(b)(1) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): when regaining the 3 take-offs, approaches and landings under the supervision of an instructor, no passengers should be on board.
- AMC1 FCL.060(b)(5) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): lists five groups of non-complex helicopter types with similar handling for which (b)(5) cross-type recency applies (e.g. Group 5: the SEP types in AMC1 FCL.740.H(b) plus R22 and R44).
- FCL.060(a) (balloons) is no longer in Part-FCL; balloon recency is BFCL.160. Gyroplanes were added to (b) by Regulation (EU) 2025/134 (applicable from 18 February 2026).

## FCL.1025 Validity, revalidation and renewal of examiner certificates

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-1025.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.1025 (ED Decision 2018/009/R): examiner refresher course follows the standardisation course content of AMC1 FCL.1015.
- GM1 FCL.1015(a); FCL.1025(b)(2) (ED Decision 2018/009/R): sailplane and balloon examiners may take the refresher course at a DTO.

## FCL.140.A LAPL(A) — Recency requirements

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-140-a.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.140.A(a)(1)(ii) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): content of the refresher training. Does not change counting.
- AMC1 FCL.140.A(a)(2) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): the proficiency check follows the content of the skill test in AMC1 FCL.125, point (e).
- GM1 FCL.140.A (ED Decision 2025/002/R): recommends the safety awareness briefing of GM2 FCL.740.A.
- FCL.035(a)(4): hours flown in Annex I (non-EASA, e.g. ultralight) aeroplanes/TMGs of the same category and class are credited toward the flight time requirements of (a)(1).

## FCL.140.H LAPL(H) – Recency requirements

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-140-h.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.140.H(a)(1)(ii), AMC1 FCL.140.H(a)(2) and GM1 FCL.140.H (ED Decision 2025/002/R) mirror the LAPL(A) material (refresher content; proficiency check follows the LAPL(H) skill test). They do not change counting.

## FCL.240.G GPL – Recency requirements

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-240-g.md`.

- FCL.035(a)(5): hours in Annex I gyroplanes with MTOM of at least 450 kg are credited toward the 12 hours and 12 take-offs and landings, but not toward the refresher training in (a)(1)(ii).

## FCL.625 IR — Validity, revalidation and renewal

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-625.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.625(c) (ED Decision 2017/022/R): renewal refresher training at an ATO is determined case by case and documented by a training completion certificate.

## FCL.710 Class and type ratings — variants

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-710.md`.

- GM1 FCL.710 and AMC1 FCL.710(a) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): distinguish differences training from familiarisation and set differences-training content for SEP variants with an electric engine.

## FCL.740.A Revalidation of class and type ratings — aeroplanes

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-740-a.md`.

- FCL.010 defines 'Route sector' as a flight comprising take-off, departure, cruise of not less than 15 minutes, arrival, approach and landing phases; this governs what counts toward (a)(2)(i).
- AMC1 FCL.740.A(b)(1)(ii)(C) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): content of the 1-hour refresher training (briefing on TEM, navigation, stall recovery; engine-failure and stall exercises). Does not change counting.
- GM1 FCL.740.A (ED Decision 2022/014/R): an EBT practical assessment is assessed across the validity period; only the administrative completion falls within the 3 months before expiry.
- GM2 FCL.740.A (ED Decision 2025/002/R): recommended safety awareness briefing of at least 15 minutes before refresher training or a proficiency check. Does not change counting.
- FCL.035(a)(4): hours flown in Annex I (non-EASA, e.g. ultralight) aeroplanes/TMGs of the same category and class are credited toward the flight time requirements of (b)(1)(ii).

## FCL.740.H Revalidation of type ratings – helicopters

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-740-h.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.740.H(b) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): only the SEP helicopter types listed there (e.g. Bell 47, Brantley B2, Enstrom F28, Cabri G2, Hiller UH12, R44, HU269) may be credited under (b); other SEP helicopters, for example the R22, are not credited.
- AMC1 FCL.740.H(a)(2)(ii)(B) and GM1 FCL.740.H (ED Decision 2025/002/R) exist; they concern refresher/briefing content and do not change counting.

## FCL.740 Validity and renewal of class and type ratings

Source text: `sources/easa/fcl-740.md`.

- AMC1 FCL.740(b) (ED Decision 2025/002/R): renewal refresher training is determined case by case (experience, time since privileges last used, complexity) and documented by a training completion certificate presented to the examiner.
- GM1 FCL.740(b) (ED Decision 2021/002/R): AOC holders may provide renewal refresher training for EBT-enrolled pilots whose rating lapsed by no more than 1 year.

## MED.A.030 Medical certificates

Source text: `sources/easa/med-a-030.md`.

- AMC1 MED.A.030 (ED Decision 2019/002/R): a class 1 medical certificate includes the privileges and validities of class 2 and LAPL medical certificates; a class 2 includes those of a LAPL medical certificate. This makes the effective validity depend on the privilege exercised, not only the certificate class.
